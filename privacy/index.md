# Privacy Policy

**App:** Trillypops (iPhone; bundle ID app.hsnbyhn.mergedrop)
**Responsible person (data controller):** Hasan Bayhan, the individual developer who publishes Trillypops
**Contact:** trillypops.app@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 7 November 2026 (applies to version 1.0.0)

## The short version
Trillypops does not collect personal data. There is no account and no sign-in. Your game progress stays on your device.

## What stays on your device
The game saves your best scores, level progress, the pieces you have found in the Codex, your chosen skin and your settings (sound, haptics, tier numbers, reduce motion, reduce transparency, relaxed tempo). This is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Apple's own device backups (iCloud or computer backups) may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. That is between you and Apple; we do not receive it.

## What this version does not do
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising and no advertising identifier.
- No analytics, tracking or profiling.
- No in-app purchases.
- No data sold or shared with anyone.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

## Third-party components
The game is built with the Unity game engine. In this version the game itself makes no network requests. Apple may collect information about app downloads, crashes and usage under your own Apple settings (for example "Share with App Developers"); Apple's privacy policy covers that. From Apple we only receive crash reports and summary statistics that do not identify you.

## Children
Trillypops is a casual puzzle game for a general audience. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights
Under laws such as the EU and UK GDPR you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because this version does not collect personal data, we hold none about you. You can remove everything the game saved by deleting the app. For any privacy question or request, contact Hasan Bayhan at trillypops.app@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Future versions
Future versions may add features such as advertising or optional purchases. Some of these may involve processing data (for example by an advertising partner). Before any such version is released, we will update this policy to say exactly what is collected, by whom and why, and update the App Store privacy information. We will also tell you about the change in the app or on the App Store listing. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version of this policy.
