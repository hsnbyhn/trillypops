# Trillypops Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email **trillypops.app@gmail.com**. We read every message; replies may take a few days.

## Frequently asked

**Which devices are supported?**
iPhone with iOS 15 or later. Trillypops is made for iPhone; there is no iPad or Android version.

**Is my progress saved online? What happens if I delete the app?**
Progress (best scores, levels, Codex, settings) is saved only on your iPhone. There is no account and no online save, so deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move to a new phone by itself.

**How do I turn sound or haptics off?**
Open Settings from the main menu and switch Sound or Haptics off. The same screen has Tier numbers, Reduce motion, Reduce transparency and Relaxed tempo.

**How do I change the look of the pieces?**
Settings > Skin. Choose Classic or Sweet Shop. The new look applies from your next game.

**How do I report a bug?**
Email trillypops.app@gmail.com with:
- your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About),
- the app version (shown at the bottom of the main menu),
- what you did, what happened and what you expected,
- a screenshot or screen recording if you can.

**The game feels slow on my phone.**
Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version. Closing other apps or restarting the phone often helps.
